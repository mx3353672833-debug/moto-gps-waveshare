# DO NOT ORDER / do not order directly

This is the engineering review package for MOTO GPS Rev A, used to confirm the real copper, drill files, placement coordinates and structural density; it is not a JLCPCB board-release package. ERC, DRC, unconnected items and schematic/PCB consistency can be zero, but the following physical items are not yet complete:

- physical confirmation of the original screen's FPC contact face, insertion direction, thickness and Pin 1;
- confirmation of USB-C against the enclosure opening, the PCB Z height and the plug envelope;
- second-person pin-by-pin sign-off of every in-house footprint;
- charge/buck-boost power coupon and sealed temperature-rise testing;
- GNSS/Wi-Fi antenna, impedance stack-up and complete-product RF testing;
- 3D/physical interference check of the battery, screen, buttons, screws and Garmin mount on the complete product.

`review_gerbers_DO_NOT_ORDER/` is for CAM/DFM pre-review only. This directory deliberately does not generate a Gerber ZIP that can be uploaded directly; the formal files can only be generated by `fab_release.py export` once all gates are closed.
