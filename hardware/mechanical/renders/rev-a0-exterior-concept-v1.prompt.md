# Rev A0 exterior concept render prompt

Use case: product-mockup

Asset type: photorealistic industrial-design exterior rendering for the MOTO GPS prototype

Input images: the rugged round navigation-device visual reference and navigation screen hierarchy; the motorcycle handlebar scale/use reference; the actual Rev A0 mechanical packaging reference, which governs proportions.

Primary request: Render an original compact round motorcycle navigation puck installed on a black motorcycle handlebar, viewed from a close three-quarter front angle so the front face, body thickness, plastic rear housing, and rear quarter-turn mount are all understandable.

Product geometry: approximately 60 mm diameter main circular body, 19 mm total thickness, 48.16 mm circular cover glass, 52 mm internal PCB. Dark graphite CNC 6061 aluminum front bezel, matte black RF-transparent PC/ABS rear shell, precise narrow seams, four small recessed black fasteners, one discreet right-side power button, downward-facing USB-C silicone cover. At 12 o'clock include an honest but visually integrated low-profile rounded black plastic GNSS antenna crown/pod, about 18 × 14 × 8 mm, blending cleanly into the rear shell rather than pretending the antenna is inside full aluminum. No speaker holes.

Screen: edge-to-edge black circular AMOLED navigation UI inspired by the reference but original and clean. A thick high-contrast pale route line curves ahead, fixed white triangular vehicle arrow points upward, large simple right-turn icon with “300 m”, small red-bordered “70” speed-limit circle. No map tiles, no brand logo, no decorative dashboard text.

Style/medium: premium photorealistic 3D product visualization, plausible manufacturable details, realistic metal bead-blast texture, satin plastic, subtle glass reflections, waterproof consumer-electronics fit and finish.

Composition/framing: device dominates the image, mounted naturally on the center section of a motorcycle handlebar; shallow depth of field with a dark neutral garage/outdoor background; enough angle to show thickness and mount without obscuring the screen.

Lighting/mood: soft overcast daylight plus controlled product rim light, restrained and technical, not cyberpunk.

Constraints: preserve the real compact proportions from the mechanical reference; original design, no brand names, no trademarks, no watermark, no captions, no extra controls, no speaker grille, no impossible transparent parts, no oversized smartwatch look, no full-aluminum enclosure around the antenna.
